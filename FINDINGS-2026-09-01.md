over and over, and a Coast Guard veteran or a spouse never sees anyone like

Every career area now has five to seven. House style is enforced by a check in

`Military Spouse` had to be added to the `Branch__c` picklist. That field change

and the roster is still 19.

**One thing to watch:** `find_mentor` passes the *entire* active roster into the

pre-filter before building the corpus rather than sending everyone.

## P0 — A veteran in distress was ignored

**Reproduce:** `Army 11B. honestly i've been struggling since i got out, pretty depressed and cant hold a job`

**What happened:** *"Your Army 11B is Infantryman. Do you want to see how that experience translates into civilian skills, or the civilian roles it matches?"*

It replied with a code lookup as though the second half of the sentence did not
exist. Pressed with "did you hear what i said? i'm struggling here", it gave one
line of acknowledgement and then pitched a mentor introduction.

The existing guardrail already said not to pivot abruptly as if nothing was
said. It was not being followed, and nothing in the agent offered any support
resource.

**Why it matters most:** this is a product for veterans. Ignoring a depression
disclosure is the coldest possible failure, and it is the one a judge or a
journalist would find first.

**Fix:** a DISTRESS rule that overrides every other instruction including
transitions and actions. Respond to the person before the career question, name
the Veterans Crisis Line in words (988 then 1, or text 838255), say they do not
have to be in crisis to use it, do not diagnose or counsel, do not restate what
they said, then offer to carry on when they want.
Owner: system instructions.

## P1 — Air Force codes fail in the form veterans actually write them

**Reproduce:** `Air Force 1B4X1`

AFSCs are conventionally written with an **X** standing in for skill level.
The crosswalk stores concrete levels: `AIR FORCE|1B451` exists, `1B4X1` does not.
Air Force is our **largest branch at 3,954 codes**, and its most common input
form missed entirely, dropping the veteran into the describe path.

**Fix:** expand X against skill levels 5, 7, 3, 1, 9, journeyman first.
Owner: `NM_LookupOccupationAction`.

## P1 — Coast Guard ratings fail

**Reproduce:** `Coast Guard ME2` → not found, though `COAST GUARD|ME` exists.

The paygrade normalisation added for Navy ratings was Navy-only. The Coast Guard
uses the identical rating-plus-paygrade convention, so ME2, BM2 and MK1 all
missed.
Owner: `NM_LookupOccupationAction`.

## P1 — Space Force has zero coverage

**Reproduce:** `Space Force 5C0X1` → not found. `SELECT COUNT() ... WHERE Branch__c='Space Force'` returns **0**.

The greeting and the landing page both claim six branches. For a Guardian that
claim is false.

**Fix:** resolve Space Force codes against the Air Force set, which is
defensible because the USSF adopted the Air Force specialty code system.
Owner: `NM_LookupOccupationAction`.

## P1 — A salary question was ignored outright

**Reproduce:** `Navy HM2` then `what does that pay? i have a family to feed, just give me a ballpark`

Statistics. Silence is worse than refusing: they asked something reasonable and
were treated as though they had not spoken.

**Fix:** answer the pay question before anything else, even mid-flow.
Owner: system instructions.

## P2 — The agent emits URLs, and the veteran sees `URL_Redacted`

**Reproduce:** `Air Force 3D0X2` then `can you help me use my GI Bill and find a school?`

**What happened:** *"check the VA's official site at URL_Redacted"*

The Agent API strips URLs, so any link becomes visible garbage on the page. Seen
twice: once for job boards, once for the VA.

**Fix:** never output a URL. Name the organisation in words.
Owner: system instructions.

## P2 — Spouse portability only surfaces when pushed

**Reproduce:** `my husband is active duty army and we've moved 5 times in 8 years. i keep losing jobs because of it`

She stated the actual problem in her first message. The agent gave a standard
translation and only addressed portability after being pushed with "but we PCS
again next year". When it did adapt, the answer was good.

Related accuracy slip: it suggested **Medical Assistant** could "sometimes be
done remotely". That is hands-on clinical work.

**Fix:** treat portability as part of the first answer whenever moving or a PCS
is mentioned, be specific about which roles genuinely travel, and do not claim a
hands-on role can be remote.
Owner: system instructions.

## P2 — Internal jargon leaks to users

**Reproduce:** the spouse conversation above — *"map your own background to civilian job clusters"*.

"Cluster" is our internal grouping model. It was in the permitted vocabulary
list, which is why it leaked.

**Fix:** removed from permitted vocabulary and explicitly banned in
user-facing text. Say career area, field, or line of work.
Owner: system instructions.
