## The four open findings collapse to three root causes

### 1. The free-text path still uses the old nine-cluster model

The **coded** path was migrated to O*NET occupations (NMDH-22) and works well.
The **free-text** path still classifies into nine hand-built clusters, each with
a fixed set of job descriptions.

That is why a Navy nuclear reactor operator lands in construction and an avionics
technician is offered Commercial Airline Pilot. The occupation they need exists
in `NM_Occupation__c` and the cluster they were put in does not contain it.

Covers F1, F5, and the same-family F6, F7, F8, F12, F13.

**Verified:** `NM_Occupation__c` holds Avionics Technicians 49-2091.00 and Nuclear
Power Reactor Operators 51-8011.00. Neither is reachable from free text.

### 2. Military-only SOC codes are presented as civilian occupations

The 55-30xx SOC family is military-only by definition. Army 11B and Marine 0311
map to 55-3016.00 "Infantry", which is returned to the veteran as their civilian
match. There is no civilian job called Infantry.

Two separable parts: the **data** (some codes map only to a military SOC, which is
honest scarcity) and the **agent behaviour** (presenting it as a civilian answer
rather than saying plainly that this code has no direct civilian mapping and
switching to the skills path).

Covers F4, and the same-family F9, F10, F15, F17.

### 3. Mentor repetition survives on the free-text path

The mentor subagent was split so the consent turn physically cannot re-describe a
mentor. That holds on the **coded** path. The D02-E repro goes through
**free-text** and still repeats, so the router guard does not cover that route.
